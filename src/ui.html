Set up in one click

holder-kit keeps your wallet keys encrypted on this computer and signs locally. Nothing is sent to anyone but the blockchain RPC you choose.

  1. Create your encrypted vault with a strong random password saved to vault/PASSWORD.txt.
  2. Make your wallets. Choose how many below — you can add more later.
  3. Write a backup and check the chain is reachable.

Afterwards, copy vault/PASSWORD.txt and a backup of the vault somewhere safe — anyone with both has your keys.
